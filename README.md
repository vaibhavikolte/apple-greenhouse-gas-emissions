# Apple Greenhouse Gas Emissions Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents a five-page Power BI dashboard for exploring greenhouse gas emissions, emission sources, product carbon footprints, and sustainability-related business indicators.

The dashboard combines key performance indicators (KPIs), charts, comparisons, and filters to help users explore emission-related information across fiscal years and product categories.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze gross emissions, carbon removal, and net emissions.
- Explore emission trends across fiscal years.
- Compare emissions by category, type, scope, and source.
- Examine carbon footprints across iPhone models and release years.
- Explore relationships between emissions, revenue, employees, and market capitalization.
- Present sustainability-related data through interactive visualizations.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
Summarizes gross emissions, carbon removal, net emissions, total revenue, average product carbon footprint, and emission trends by fiscal year.

### 2. Emission Analysis
Explores emissions by category and type, emission trends by fiscal year, and the leading emission sources represented in the dataset.

### 3. Scope and Source Analysis
Presents year-wise scope analysis, emission source information, and comparisons between corporate emissions and product life-cycle emissions.

### 4. Product Carbon Footprint
Compares carbon footprint indicators across iPhone models and release years, including average, highest, and lowest product footprint values shown in the dashboard.

### 5. Growth vs Sustainability
Explores year-wise comparisons involving employee counts, revenue, emissions, and market capitalization.
